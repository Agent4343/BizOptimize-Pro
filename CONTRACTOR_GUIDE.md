# Contractor Guide - Construction Estimator
## How to Use This Tool to Generate Professional Customer Quotes

---

## 🎯 **Purpose**

This tool helps **contractors** quickly and accurately generate professional construction quotes to present to customers. It ensures you:

- ✅ Ask all the right questions
- ✅ Don't miss important details
- ✅ Generate accurate, professional quotes
- ✅ Save time on calculations and formatting
- ✅ Present consistent, industry-standard quotes

---

## 📋 **Step-by-Step Process**

### **Step 1: Project Overview**
**What to ask your customer:**
- "What's your name and project address?"
- "Can you describe what you'd like done?" (one sentence)
- "Is this a new build, renovation, addition, or repair?"
- "What province/city is the project in?"
- "If it's a renovation, what year was the home built?"

**Why this matters:** Gets basic project info and location for accurate pricing.

---

### **Step 2: Work Scope (Trades)**
**What to ask your customer:**
- "What work needs to be done?" (Select all applicable trades)
- Electrical? Plumbing? Framing? Drywall? etc.

**Why this matters:** Determines which trades to include in the estimate.

---

### **Step 3: Measurements**
**What to ask your customer:**
- "What's the total square footage?"
- "Do you have room-by-room measurements?" (optional but helpful)
- "What's the ceiling height?"
- "How many floors?"

**Why this matters:** Accurate measurements = accurate costs. Consider doing a site visit to verify.

---

### **Step 4: Site Conditions**
**What to check/ask:**
- "How easy is access to the site?" (Easy/Normal/Difficult/Obstructed)
- "Are the walls open, partial, or fully finished?"
- "Any known issues?" (Old wiring, rotten framing, asbestos, mold, water damage)

**Why this matters:** Site conditions affect labor time and costs. Document everything to avoid surprises.

---

### **Step 5: Materials**
**What to discuss with customer:**
- "What material grade do you prefer?" (Basic/Standard/Premium)
- "Any preferred brands?"
- "Will you supply materials or should we?"

**Why this matters:** Material choices significantly impact cost. Discuss options with customer.

---

### **Step 6: Labor**
**What to plan:**
- How many workers do you need?
- What experience level? (Apprentice/Journeyperson/Red Seal/Master)
- Union or non-union?
- Any overtime or weekend work needed?

**Why this matters:** Labor is often the biggest cost. Plan your crew appropriately.

---

### **Step 7: Timeline**
**What to discuss with customer:**
- "When would you like to start?"
- "When do you need it completed?"
- "Will we have full access or partial access?"

**Why this matters:** Timeline affects scheduling and may require overtime costs.

---

### **Step 8: Permits, Codes & Company Info**
**What to verify:**
- "Do you know if permits are required?"
- "Who will pull the permits?" (You or customer)
- "Any code upgrades needed?"

**Fill in your company information:**
- Company name, address, phone, email
- License number
- Your name (estimator)
- Customer name
- Project address
- Quote validity period (30/60/90 days)
- Overhead and profit percentages

**Why this matters:** Permits cost money. Company info personalizes the quote.

---

## 📄 **What the Quote Includes**

Each generated quote includes:

1. **Professional Header**
   - Your company information
   - Quote number and dates
   - Customer information

2. **Three Pricing Options**
   - Budget Build (basic materials)
   - Best Value (standard materials)
   - Premium (high-end materials)

3. **Complete Cost Breakdown**
   - Trade-by-trade costs
   - Materials and labor separated
   - Overhead and profit clearly shown
   - Additional costs (permits, equipment, waste, contingency)

4. **Payment Schedule**
   - 4 milestones with percentages
   - Due dates based on timeline

5. **Terms & Conditions**
   - Payment terms
   - Change order policy
   - Warranty information
   - Quote validity

6. **Exclusions & Assumptions**
   - What's NOT included
   - Assumptions made

7. **CSI MasterFormat Line Items**
   - Industry-standard line items
   - Quantities and unit costs
   - Detailed breakdowns

8. **Material Specifications**
   - Specific brands/models
   - Quality grades

9. **Timeline**
   - Start and completion dates
   - Critical path

10. **Building Code Requirements**
    - Provincial requirements
    - Permit information

## 💡 **Best Practices**

### **Before Meeting with Customer:**
1. Review the 8-step questionnaire
2. Prepare questions to ask
3. Consider doing a site visit first

### **During Customer Meeting:**
1. Fill out the form as you gather information
2. Ask follow-up questions if needed
3. Document everything (site conditions, access, etc.)

### **After Generating Quote:**
1. Review all three options
2. Check that costs are reasonable
3. Verify company information is correct
4. Print or email quote to customer
5. Follow up within quote validity period

### **Presenting to Customer:**
1. Explain the three options clearly
2. Highlight payment schedule
3. Point out exclusions to avoid misunderstandings
4. Emphasize quote expiration date
5. Be ready to answer questions

---

## ⚠️ **Important Notes**

- **Quote Validity:** Quotes expire (default 90 days). Update if needed.
- **Site Visits:** Always recommended for accurate estimates
- **Permits:** Verify with local building department
- **Material Prices:** May fluctuate - quote expiration protects you
- **Change Orders:** Document all changes in writing
- **Exclusions:** Clearly explain what's NOT included
